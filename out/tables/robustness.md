## Notes on each

- **landmark k = 3** — less time for a verdict to arrive before classification. Moves the difference by -3.2 pp.
- **landmark k = 14** — more time for a verdict to arrive before classification. Moves the difference by +3.3 pp.
- **excluding not-misleading first notes** — these notes are structurally barred from Helpful; promoted to a headline robustness number at Gate 1. Moves the difference by +1.0 pp.
- **retention = two or more later notes** — a stricter definition of still contributing. Moves the difference by +9.3 pp.
- **cohorts with 90+ days observed after the window** — drops the most recent cohorts entirely. Moves the difference by -0.0 pp.
